3Method

  • PayloadinstrumentId|sensorType|value|unit|capturedAt, UTF-8, no spaces. A field holding the bar or a control character fails.
  • Fingerprintrn = SHA-256 of the payload (FIPS 180-4), lowercase hex.
  • Linkhn = SHA-256 of the text hn−1 + rn + n, from 64 zeros. A changed value breaks only the link into the next record.
  • SignatureECDSA P-256 over rn (FIPS 186-5), IEEE P1363, low-S only. A missing signature fails. Records 1 to {{AN}} were signed at build with a discarded demo key. Demo key id {{keyId}} (first 16 bytes of SHA-256 of its SPKI). Public key, uncompressed point:{{pub}}
  • AnchorThe replayed link of record {{AN}} against the anchored fingerprint on this page (simulated, no network call). A correction stays “not yet anchored”.
  • PassFingerprint and link match and the signature does not fail. On an http:// page a built-in SHA-256 recomputes fingerprints and links, signatures are not checked, and no record is called unchanged.
  • LimitsThe key and the anchor come with the page, so this shows the method, not independent evidence. A removed record with every later link rewritten passes every check but the anchor. A correction’s one-time key travels with it.
