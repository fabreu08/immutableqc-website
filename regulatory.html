Regulatory map

1Scope

Built toward the audit-trail expectations of 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11 and ISO/IEC 17025:2017. Not validated. No electronic signatures yet. Not a substitute for your computerized-system validation or SOPs.

Each expectation in plain words, the approach, what exists today and what is not covered. Clause numbers appear only where confirmed. A reading aid, not a claim that anything meets an expectation; check your own edition.

LegendToday means the open alpha: these pages or the alpha app (not public). Not covered lists what Immutable QC does not do; items tagged Upcoming are in the Roadmap, and the rest are not. All demo data is synthetic.

221 CFR Part 11

2.1Validation; discerning altered records §11.10(a)

Expectation
Validated systems that discern altered records.
Approach
A fingerprint, a link and a signature per record: an altered record fails and breaks the next link.
Today
These pages, on synthetic data; the alpha app seals each imported peak area.
Not covered
Validation. Server-side verification in the app Upcoming

2.2Protection of records for retrieval

Expectation
Records retrievable accurately through retention.
Approach
A retrieved record can be checked against its seal.
Today
The check only.
Not covered
Storage, backup, retention and archiving.

2.3Limiting system access

Expectation
Access limited to authorized people.
Approach
Not addressed.
Today
None; no logins, and the alpha app’s interfaces are open.
Not covered
Access control.

2.4Audit trails §11.10(e)

Expectation
Time-stamped trails of who created, changed or deleted records, keeping earlier entries.
Approach
Corrections are new records with a reason; the original stays. A removed record or rewritten history shows only against an earlier anchor (simulated here).
Today
Corrections on these pages, the reason and time beside the seal.
Not covered
Operator identity; a trusted clock (the alpha app’s capture time is an injection time typed at import, or the server’s clock); corrections in the alpha app.

2.5Controls for open systems

Expectation
Encryption and signature standards for authenticity and confidentiality.
Approach
ECDSA P-256 (FIPS 186-5) over each fingerprint; nothing is sent over a network.
Today
Demo keys; one server key in the alpha app.
Not covered
Managed keys, encryption. Per-analyst signatures Upcoming

2.6Signature manifestations and linking §11.50, §11.70

Expectation
Signer, time and meaning shown; signatures bound to their records.
Approach
A signature covers exactly the sealed fields.
Today
A signature made with a system key on a record’s bytes is neither an electronic signature nor a digital signature as 21 CFR Part 11 uses those terms: both identify a person.
Not covered
Signer, time and meaning. Reviewer sign-off Upcoming

2.7Electronic signatures

Expectation
Unique to one verified person.
Approach
A key alone is not an electronic signature.
Today
None.
Not covered
Electronic signatures.
