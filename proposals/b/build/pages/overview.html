2How a record is sealed

The alpha seals five fields of each result: instrument, measurement type, value, unit and capture time; sample and method metadata inside the seal are on the roadmap. Each record’s SHA-256 fingerprint is linked to the previous record’s, which makes the record history tamper-evident: a change anywhere breaks that link and every later one. A correction is a new record with a reason; the original stays.

BasisSignatures: ECDSA P-256, FIPS 186-5.

3Check a record

Change any sealed field of the eight demo records and your browser recomputes the fingerprint, checks the signature and replays the links as you type, with the shell commands to repeat each step outside the browser. Then try to hide the change, or correct it in the open.

5Roadmap

Today there is a browser demo on a synthetic lab and an alpha app that imports HPLC CSV files. Planned: verified experiment data that AI agents can check before they use it, per-analyst signatures, sample and method metadata inside the sealed record, a shared registry, and Filecoin integration.
