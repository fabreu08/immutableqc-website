About

1Who builds it

Immutable QC is an independent project by José A. Fernández Abreu, built on his own time. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any employer.

joseqc.com

2Status

Independent project · Open alpha · Public test network · Synthetic demo data · No customers yet. Nothing on this site is validated for regulated use.

3Why

Undocumented work did not happen. Labs already keep audit trails; the aim is a record that someone outside the lab can check from the record itself: a reviewer, an auditor, or later an AI agent.
