Regulatory map

1Scope

Built toward the audit-trail expectations of 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11 and ISO/IEC 17025:2017. Not validated. No electronic signatures yet. Not a substitute for your computerized-system validation or SOPs.

This map says, clause by clause, what the open alpha shows and what it does not do. It is an outline; check every clause against your own copy of the text.

221 CFR Part 11

§11.10(a), validation of systems, including the ability to discern invalid or altered records: the demo shows how an altered record is discerned, by its fingerprint, signature and links; the system is not validated. §11.10(e), time-stamped audit trails in which changes do not obscure earlier entries: in the browser demos a correction is appended with a reason and the original stays. On this site the reason and correction time sit beside the seal, not inside it, and the alpha app has no corrections yet. There is no operator identity yet, and the capture time comes from the import, not a trusted clock. §11.50, §11.70 and Subpart C, electronic signatures: not provided.

3EU GMP Annex 11 (2011)

§9, a record of GMP-relevant changes with the reason documented: the demo appends corrections with a reason; on this site the reason and correction time sit beside the seal, not inside it. Validation (§4), security and access control (§12) and electronic signature (§14): not provided. A revised Annex 11 has been in consultation; check the numbering against the edition you work to.

4ISO/IEC 17025:2017

7.5.2, amendments tracked to the original, which is retained with the date, the altered aspect and the person responsible: corrections are appended and the original stays, with the correction time beside the seal; who made the change is not recorded yet. 7.11.3, data systems safeguarded against tampering and loss: the alpha makes the record history tamper-evident; it does not prevent loss or control access.

5ALCOA+ and GMP data integrity

A fingerprint shows that a record has not changed since it was sealed. It cannot show that the result was right when it was captured. Attributable needs per-analyst signatures, and original needs the source file sealed with the result: both are on the roadmap.

6Environmental labs (TNI)

Chain of custody, holding times and method blanks are outside the alpha.
