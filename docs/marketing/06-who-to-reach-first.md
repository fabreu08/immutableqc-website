# Who to reach first: 20 kinds of labs and conferences

Automated research labs first, then regulated QC labs. No company names. Order is a suggestion: the closer a lab is
to AI agents choosing the next experiment, the sooner it feels the problem.

## Labs (12)

1. Self-driving labs in materials science, where an AI agent picks the next experiment from the last result.
2. Autonomous chemistry labs running closed-loop synthesis and optimization.
3. High-throughput screening cores in biotech, with plate readers writing many files a day.
4. Automated microbiology labs: growth curves, antimicrobial testing, colony imaging.
5. Protein engineering and directed-evolution labs with automated design, build and test cycles.
6. Synthetic biology foundries running strain construction at scale.
7. Battery and electrochemistry research labs with automated cycling rigs.
8. Catalysis and reaction-screening labs that use robotic liquid handlers.
9. University core facilities that share instruments across many groups and need clean handoffs.
10. Academic AI-for-science groups building lab agents and needing an audit trail for them.
11. Contract research labs that deliver data folders to sponsors.
12. Biotech startups with a new automation stack and no legacy data platform yet.

## Conferences and gatherings (8)

13. Lab automation and screening conferences.
14. Self-driving lab and autonomous experimentation workshops.
15. AI-for-science and machine-learning-for-chemistry workshops at machine learning conferences.
16. Materials research society meetings, especially the autonomous materials discovery sessions.
17. Chemistry society national meetings, in the sections on lab automation and data.
18. Synthetic biology and bioengineering conferences.
19. Research data management and FAIR data meetings for core facility managers.
20. Data integrity forums for QC and quality professionals (for the later regulated audience).
