What We Do

Machine‑Signed Truth: The IQC Middleware

Immutable QC (IQC) is a high‑integrity middleware layer that bridges the gap between physical analytical instruments and any digital ledger. We ensure your data is born compliant.

Your laboratory ecosystem — HPLC, Mass Spectrometers, pH meters, and environmental sensors — connects directly to an IQC instance. As measurements occur, IQC cryptographically signs and commits the data directly to an immutable audit trail.

The Pipeline

From Instrument to Immutable Record

1

Connect

Your laboratory instruments — HPLC, Mass Spectrometers, pH meters, and environmental sensors — connect directly to an IQC instance.

2

Capture

As measurements occur, IQC captures the data at the moment of creation — no manual transcription, no gap between instrument and record.

3

Sign & Commit

Each measurement is cryptographically signed and committed directly to the blockchain, creating an unbroken chain of custody.

4

Verify

Auditors, regulators, and stakeholders verify data integrity in real time — without ever questioning the record.

Strategic Milestones

Roadmap

In Progress

Phase 1: Core Pipeline

Now — Q3 2026
  • Source-to-Ledger Capture: End-to-end flow for capturing measurements at the instrument, applying cryptographic signatures, and committing tamper-evident records to a hash-chained ledger + on-chain registry.
  • QC Packet Schema & Review Workflow: Define the core data model for packaging readings with metadata, operator sign-off, and reviewer attestations. Ship the minimal reviewer interface for approval/rejection with full audit trail.
  • Guardrailed AI Assistance: Add AI-assisted parsing and normalization for common lab exports (starting with HPLC), with strong validation and clear "cannot process" behavior when confidence is low.
  • Core Instrument Support: Reliable direct-to-ledger support for the most common environmental and analytical sensors (pH, temperature, conductivity, HPLC), including calibration metadata.
  • Staking & On-Chain Commitments: Make the staking requirement and on-chain commit flow reliable and understandable for users who need immutable, third-party verifiable records.
Upcoming

Phase 2: Scale & Integration

Q4 2026 – Q1 2027
  • LIMS / ELN Integrations: Production connectors to common lab systems (Benchling, LabWare, etc.) so verified IQC results flow into existing workflows without duplicate entry.
  • Public API & Developer Platform: Stable REST API + webhooks for ingesting data, retrieving QC packets, and verifying provenance programmatically.
  • Broader Instrument Support: Validated support for the most common analytical and environmental instruments beyond the initial set, with consistent metadata capture.
  • Multi-Site & Team Features: Role-based access, team workspaces, and basic governance controls for labs running IQC across multiple sites or departments.
  • Production AI Pipeline: Harden and scale the guardrailed AI features from Phase 1 for real lab throughput, with clear accuracy metrics and human oversight loops.
Future

Phase 3: Advanced Capabilities

2027+
  • Real-time Monitoring Agents: Optional edge components that can stream live instrument data and flag obvious anomalies or calibration drift as it happens.
  • Advanced Analytics & Risk Views: Aggregated dashboards and trend analysis across instruments, methods, and sites to help labs spot systemic issues earlier.
  • Stronger Cryptographic Verification: Explore selective disclosure and zero-knowledge techniques so external parties can verify integrity without accessing raw proprietary data.
  • Continuous Improvement Loop: Use anonymized, permissioned data from participating labs to improve detection models and best-practice recommendations over time.
