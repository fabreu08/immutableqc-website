Laboratory Data That Cannot Be Altered

Signed at the source. A permanent, auditable record that can't be altered — with privacy controls when you need them.

Immutable QC (IQC) is middleware that captures measurements analytical instruments such as HPLC, MS, Discrete Analyzers, Flow Injection, & Environmental Sensors then signs them cryptographically and commits tamper-evident records to an immutable audit trail. The Problem

Laboratory Data Is Vulnerable at Every Step

Manual Transcription Errors

Data moves from instrument to LIMS via manual entry or fragile CSV exports. Errors propagate. Values change. The chain of custody breaks before it begins.

Undetectable Tampering

Without cryptographic verification, altered records are indistinguishable from authentic ones. Auditors must trust process. They cannot verify integrity mathematically.

Compliance Overhead

21 CFR Part 11 and ALCOA+ requirements demand complete, consistent, enduring records. Traditional systems generate paper trails, not mathematical proofs.

FDA Warning Letters from 2023 to 2024 show data integrity violations as the leading cause of regulatory action against analytical laboratories.

The Solution

Cryptographic Signing at the Source

IQC sits between your instruments and your data systems. It captures measurements the moment they are generated, before human hands touch them, applying cryptographic signatures and committing tamper-evident records to a hash-chained ledger with on-chain attestation.

Source-to-Ledger Provenance

Every measurement is signed at the instrument using ECDSA. The signature, timestamp, and instrument metadata form an unbreakable chain of custody from capture to audit.

Zero Manual Transcription

Direct instrument integration eliminates the gap between measurement and record. No CSV exports. No copy-paste errors. No opportunity for manipulation.

Mathematical Audit Trails

Regulators verify integrity via cryptographic proof, not process documentation. Hash-chained records with on-chain attestation provide third-party verifiable evidence of data integrity.

How It Works

From Instrument to Immutable Record

  1. 01

    Connect

    HPLC, mass spectrometers, pH meters, and environmental sensors connect via standardized protocols (RS-232, TCP/IP, file watchers).

  3. 02

    Capture

    Raw measurement data is intercepted at the point of generation, before it reaches any editable system. Calibration metadata is captured simultaneously.

  5. 03

    Sign & Commit

    IQC applies ECDSA signatures to each measurement, appends to a local hash-chained ledger (SQLite-backed), and submits a commitment to the on-chain registry.

  7. 04

    Verify

    Auditors verify integrity via public attestation records. Cryptographic proofs confirm data has not been altered since capture, without accessing proprietary raw data.

Technical Implementation

Built on Proven Cryptographic Standards

IQC implements established cryptographic attestation patterns from supply chain security and distributed systems research, adapted for laboratory compliance requirements.

Research Foundation

Sigstore

Certificate transparency and keyless signing infrastructure. IQC adapts Sigstore patterns for short-lived signing keys and transparent attestation logs.

sigstore.dev →

OpenTimestamps

Bitcoin-backed timestamping for proof of existence. IQC uses OpenTimestamps-style anchoring for third-party verifiable timestamps without blockchain bloat.

opentimestamps.org →

CLSI AUTO-10A

Automated verification standards for clinical laboratories. IQC aligns with CLSI AUTO-10A guidelines for automated result verification and exception handling.
